# Contributing

Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md), then STATE → involved profiles → roles → [lessons index](lessons/README.md) → open proposals. Quality of outcome and explanation comes before token savings.

Lessons hold general principles, not cases or behavior changes. Before recording, judge whether an observation recurs across projects, models, and agents; if not, record no lesson or class entry. For a general observation, identify the fundamental problem and refine its existing lesson or add `lessons/<problem>.md` using the template. Use the [proposal template](proposals/README.md) for front/role decisions.

One incident, one record: dated agent-specific evidence goes in that agent's profile, general principles in lessons, operational facts where used; no rebuttal records. Weaknesses require two independent incidents or an explicit human statement; single incidents remain dated profile evidence. Profile claims carry dated evidence inline or quote the human.

After delivery, use one short background Writer call only for a new general principle or a refinement; otherwise skip. Lessons, small status updates, and model-catalog facts may go directly to main after review. Anything changing how agents work needs human approval first, then a branch/PR and human-approved merge. Fold an approved general lesson into a playbook rule through a PR, then mark it `promoted` with rule links. Unapproved suggestions remain lessons.

Workers never commit/push. Front reviews facts, English prose, scope, and privacy before publication. Maintenance never blocks delivery.
