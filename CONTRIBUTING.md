# Contributing

Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md), the operating policy, then [STATE.md](STATE.md) for validation and open proposals. Quality of outcome and explanation comes before token savings.

Use the [lesson template](lessons/README.md) for facts and [proposal template](proposals/README.md) for front/role decisions. Lessons, small status updates, and model-catalog facts may go directly to main. Anything changing how agents work needs human approval first, then a branch/PR and human-approved merge. Unapproved improvements remain lessons.

Workers never commit/push. Front reviews facts, English prose, scope, and privacy before publication. Maintenance never blocks delivery.
