# Cold-read prompt (template)

You are a cold reader: a capable engineer who has NOT seen any background material. Read only the REPORT supplied with this prompt once. Answer briefly in the report's language. Do not use outside knowledge or run commands.

For unclear words or explanations, flag a problem only if the item's own text (its bold sentence plus its paragraph) does not resolve it; do not flag what the next sentence already explains. Check summary sentences separately: each must stand alone even if its paragraph explains it. Report flags for front triage, not automatic changes.

1. State the one-sentence conclusion.

2. List the groups and, under each, its points in order.

3. List the decisions the reader must make.

4. Quote every word or sentence you had to guess or read twice, and say why. Or "none".

5. Quote every bold summary sentence (the conclusion and each point) that cannot be understood on
   its own: it names a topic instead of a finding, or it is missing the subject, the setting, the
   key supplied number, or comparison or consequence. Say which part is missing. Do not demand
   invented numbers for unmeasured findings or open questions. Or "none".

6. Name any group whose items are not the same kind of thing, or any item placed at the wrong level.
   Or "none".

7. Name any formatting that made reading uncomfortable (line breaks, indentation, list depth,
   spacing). Or "none".
