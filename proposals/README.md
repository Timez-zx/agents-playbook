# Proposals

Record front switches and specialist-role reassignments in unique `YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md` files. Front first raises the change after delivery in one or two lines with evidence; the proposal never blocks the task. The human decides, not the agents.

## Template

```markdown
# Specific proposed change

## Proposal

Exact front switch or role reassignment.

## Evidence

Lesson links and dated owner quotes; observations separate from expectations.

## Expected benefit

What improves; how the trial measures it.

## Risks

Costs, uncertainties, possible regressions.

## Trial plan

Small reversible test, acceptance criteria, revert procedure.

## Decision

Filled by human: accepted | rejected | trial
Date: YYYY-MM-DD
```

Silence is not approval; keep `trial` open until human resolves it. A front switch does not exchange specialist roles. Follow [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) for approval, behavior-change PRs, privacy, installation, and STATE updates; a proposal decision does not bypass implementation review.
